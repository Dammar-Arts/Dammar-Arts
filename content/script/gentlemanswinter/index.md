# Gentleman's Winter

> This song was written for a game called 'The Beaumont Legacy'. It describes the thoughts of the main character, witness to obscene wealth alongside starving workers.

The velvet is drawn, the crystal catches light,  
The embers gleam against the winter's bite.  
The heavy wool rests, the parlor is warm,  
A pane of cut glass between us and the storm.

**CHOIR**

Pins on the map, pink and blue.

Buildings made of brick and steel.

Men do what they have to do.

Starve or work for a meal.

**CHOIR**

Boardroom lions, blue collars, blood of the machine.

Wealthy scions or covered in tar, we're both seventeen.

It drinks up the river, it swallows the coal,

A steady machine with a hole for a soul.

**CHOIR**

I carried the gauze and I carried the weight,

I stood in the trench and I argued with fate.

Now I sit in the study and sign on the line,

While the boiler room bleeds just to pour them the wine.

**CHOIR**

Let the fire burn quiet. Let the winter stay blind.

A gentleman leaves all the starving behind.

Strike the iron. Turn the wheel.

Smile at the cold that you don't have to feel.

**SPOKEN:** I can't hear them from here.

**CHOIR:**

And they smile at the rhythm, they sway to the hum,

They don't fear the cold, way beneath their post.

From the high tower, you don't notice the slum,

But I hear the breathing of men we have lost.
